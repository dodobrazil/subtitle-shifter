How to convert ASS to SRT

  1. Choose your .ass or .ssa file, or paste its text into the box.
  2. Pick the Encoding if accented letters look wrong. If the file has several styles, tick the ones you want; signs and songs start unticked.
  3. Press Download .srt. The file keeps the original name with the .srt extension.

What changes: ASS stores each line as a Dialogue: entry with style, margins and override tags such as {\an8} or {\i1}. SRT only has a number, a time range and text, so layout and effects are dropped. A \N becomes a line break.

FAQ

How do I convert ASS to SRT?

Choose your .ass file or paste its text and press Download. Each dialogue line becomes a numbered SRT cue with its start and end time. Nothing is uploaded.

What happens to the styling, fonts and colors?

SRT cannot store fonts, colors, positions or karaoke effects, so they are dropped. Italic and bold can be kept as <i> and <b> tags with the Keep italic and bold option, which most players understand.

Can I leave out signs and songs?

Yes. When the file uses more than one style, the tool lists every style with its line count. Styles named like Sign, Song, OP or ED start unticked; tick or untick any style to include or leave out its lines. Drawing-mode lines (vector graphics) are always skipped.

Does it also convert SSA files?

Yes. Old SSA files and newer ASS (Advanced SubStation Alpha) files use the same Dialogue lines and are detected automatically.

Why do accented letters look wrong?

The file is probably not UTF-8. Pick the matching Encoding (for example windows-1251 for Russian or windows-1254 for Turkish); the SRT is always written as UTF-8.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The conversion runs in your browser with JavaScript and your file never leaves your device.
