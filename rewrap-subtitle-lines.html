FAQ

How do I change the line length of an SRT file?

Set the maximum characters per line (42 is the common limit) and press Re-wrap. Each cue's text is joined and broken again at word boundaries so no line goes over the limit.

Why 42 characters per line?

Netflix and most broadcast style guides use 42 characters per line and at most two lines. Some projects use 37 for mobile or 32 for children's content, so the limit is adjustable.

Does it break words in the middle?

No. Lines only break between words. A single word longer than the limit stays on its own line and is reported in the status message.

What about dialogue lines that start with a dash?

If every line of a cue starts with a dash (two speakers), each speaker line is kept separate and only wrapped if it is too long.

Are italic and other tags kept?

Yes. HTML-style tags such as <i> and <b> and ASS codes such as {\an8} are kept and not counted toward the line length.

Is my file uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs in your browser and the file never leaves your device.
